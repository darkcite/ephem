          Tor
          
        
          Connecting to Tor through Snowflake…
          
        
          Your peers see an onion address, never your IP address. The Snowflake volunteer proxy and broker see your IP address and that you use Tor, not whom you talk to. Messages take 0.5–2 s. Keep this tab open, and visible in Safari, to stay reachable. For faster starts, the public Tor directory is kept on this device (no chats, peers or keys).

          Hide your IP address
          
        
          In direct mode your peer sees your IP address. Tor mode connects you through Tor instead: slower, text only, and your peer must use Tor mode too.
